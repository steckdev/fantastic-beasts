# 🎨 Fantastic Beasts GO — Sprite & Asset Prompts Guide

This document defines the visual art style standards and exact prompts for generating additional game assets, capture animations, treats, and environment backdrops for **Fantastic Beasts GO**.

---

## 🖌️ Visual Style & Consistency Rules

To ensure any new sprite seamlessly integrates with our existing 36 Fantastic Beasts and 4 Heroes:
- **Art Style**: Cute Chibi 2D fantasy RPG vector sprite art.
- **Outlines**: Clean, smooth dark line-art (around 2px - 4px width).
- **Shading**: Vibrant cel-shading with warm golden / arcane rim lighting.
- **Background**: **Always specify `isolated on pure white background`**. This allows our automated alpha flood-fill pipeline to extract 100% clean transparent PNGs without cut artifacts.
- **Aspect Ratio**: `1:1` square (minimum 1024x1024 or 2048x2048).
